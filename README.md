# Console-RNG-Game
WELCOME!

You are given a random # of health, from 49 - 100. 
You are also given a boss to fight, and you will have 3 weapons to choose from, with the third weapon having a cooldown of 5s. 

Every time you survive, you gain 10 health, and after 3 attacks, you gain a new attack which can be powerful, or not.

This game includes 2 bosses. Surviving the first one grants a random # of health to help defeat the next boss.

Good luck!
